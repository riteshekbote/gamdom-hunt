**Q5 Novel?** YES — no evidence of prior report; cross-brand shared origin is unusual architecture
**Q6 On rejected list?** NO
**Q7 Triager accept?** BORDERLINE — strong architecture flaw with high amplification, but server-side host-blind cookie acceptance unverified

**Verdict: HOLD**
Reason: High-impact hypothesis (cross-brand ATO across 4 brands/25+ hosts) but server-side cookie validation remains unverified — requires AUTH_HELPED session replay test on gamdom80008.com→gamdom.com /client-api. Passive proof insufficient; CORS blocks browser vector. Recommend human gate: authenticate, replay cookie, diff response.
